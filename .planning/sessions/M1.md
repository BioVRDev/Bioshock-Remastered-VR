## M1-S1 · Pin `PlayerController.myHUD`

**Status:** ✅ **done, 2026-08-09** — `myHUD = controller+0x71C`, backref
`HUD.PlayerOwner = +0x470`, both routes agree once `FMatrix` alignment is
accounted for. Layout recorded in `docs/ENGINE-MAP.md`. **S2 is unblocked and
its `+0x490` bHideHUD prediction is already confirmed** — see below.
· **Type:** diagnostic, no visible change · **1 cycle**

### The one idea
Find `myHUD`'s byte offset on the PlayerController, and prove it by making the HUD
object identify itself — not by trusting arithmetic.

### Why it is cheap
`CameraHook` already holds the PlayerController: it is `pThis` in the
`eventPlayerCalcView` detour (`Camera/CameraHook.cpp`, anchor `the detour`).
`myHUD` is `Engine/Classes/PlayerController.uc:115`.

### Specification — two routes, and **both must agree**

**Route A, predict.** Count declarations forward from a measured anchor in
`research/uscript/Engine/Classes/Controller.uc` and `PlayerController.uc`. Anchors
(`docs/ENGINE-MAP.md`): `AController::Pawn = +0x450`, world FOV `controller+0x45C`,
foreground FOV `+0x460`. `AActor` base is `0x450`, so Controller's own fields start
there. Sizes: `float`/`int`/pointer/object-ref 4 · `FName` 8 · `FVector` 12 ·
`FRotator` 12 · `FString`/`TArray` 12 · **`bool` packs into a shared DWORD** —
consecutive bools do *not* each cost 4. That is the trap. **Log your working** so
it can be audited.

**Route B, confirm by self-validation.** One-shot probe in `GameState_Observe`, on
a keypress (`VK_NEXT` — check collisions first, `VK_PRIOR` already has three
readers). For each 4-byte slot in a window around the prediction, accept a
candidate only if **all** hold:

1. Readable pointer into a plausible heap range.
2. First DWORD is a plausible vtable inside the module.
3. **The back-reference test** — the HUD's own `PlayerOwner`/`Owner` points back at
   the PlayerController you started from. This is what makes the result
   self-validating rather than merely plausible. (`HandsProbe` does the same
   three-stage positional identification — `docs/modules/hands.md`.)

Log every candidate with its offset and why it passed or failed. **Read only.
Write nothing.**

### Falsifiable outcome
`>>> MYHUD: predicted=+0xNNN confirmed=+0xNNN vtable=0x... backref=ok`, with the
pointer non-null and stable across several seconds of play.
**No:** zero candidates pass, or the routes disagree irreconcilably — log both
numbers and stop. Do not guess which is right.

### If it fails
Do not widen the scan and retry blind. If the prediction was clean and only the
back-reference failed, proceed to S2 with the predicted offset. Otherwise go
straight to M3, which does not need this offset at all.

### Do not
Write to engine memory · add a per-frame scan (one-shot, lock, stop) · hardcode a
raw address (offsets go through config) · revisit the pawn-side
`LastPlayerInputContext` scan (grave 6).

## M1-S2 · Read `bHideHUD` and prove it tracks cinematic mode

**Status:** ✅ **done, 2026-08-09 — the answer is NO.** The DWORD read
`0x00000020` and never changed across 16 minutes and four controller lifetimes,
including the bathysphere descent, the plasmid injection, both halves of a
Little Sister sequence, combat and barrels. It did not move even while the HUD
visibly appeared and disappeared. **Eight marked boundaries, zero transitions
at any of them** — the marker key was broken during the run, but the presses
were recovered from the `KEY: vk` keylogger in `Render/XRSession.cpp`. Evidence in `docs/INVARIANTS.md` §
*Cutscene detection*; grave 1 in `CLAUDE.md`. **→ S3 is skipped. Open M3-S1.**
· **Type:** diagnostic, no visible change · **1 cycle** · **Depends on:** S1

> **The pivotal test of the whole arc.** A clean *no* is a successful session —
> treat the result as the deliverable.

Extra context: `docs/INVARIANTS.md` § *Cutscene detection* (the eight graves).

### The one idea
Read `myHUD.bHideHUD` every observe tick, log transitions, and give the tester a
marker key.

### Specification

**1. Locate the bit — ✅ already done by S1.** The containing DWORD is
**`myHUD+0x490`** and `bHideHUD` is **bit 0**; S1's live dump read `0x00000020`
in ordinary play, i.e. only `bMessageBeep` set and `bHideHUD` correctly clear.
The full six-bool bit order and the confirmed HUD layout are in
`docs/ENGINE-MAP.md` § *HUD actor*. **Still log the whole DWORD in hex, not one
bit** — that is what makes a neighbouring bool flipping distinguishable, and it
is the point of the falsifiable outcome below.

`myHUD` itself is `controller+0x71C`. Verify identity via the back-reference at
`myHUD+0x470` before every read, as S1 did.

**2. Log on transition only.** In `GameState_Observe` (game thread, where the
existing probes live). Verify identity before every read — `myHUD` non-null, vtable
plausible, back-reference intact. **Fail closed:** on mismatch log once and stop
reading until the pawn changes.

```
>>> CINE: dword 0x00000000 -> 0x00000001  (t=123.4)
```

The raw DWORD makes a neighbouring bool flipping distinguishable from `bHideHUD`.

**3. Marker key.** `VK_END` is free. On press log `>>> ===== MARK =====`. The
tester cannot see timestamps live, so this is how a visual event gets pinned to a
log line.

**4.** No INI switch — it writes nothing. Keep log volume near zero.

### What to tell the tester
1. New game. **Press `VK_END` when the bathysphere begins and again on regaining
   control.**
2. Play to the **plasmid injection**. Mark both ends.
3. A normal combat stretch and a crate, marking nothing — the false-positive check.
4. Optionally a **Little Sister rescue**. It is *predicted not to fire* (it pushes
   `NullInput`). **A non-firing rescue confirms the model rather than breaking it.**

### Falsifiable outcome
**Yes:** a transition to set within ~a second of each opening `MARK`, back to clear
near each closing one, and **zero transitions** during combat.
**No:** the DWORD never changes, or changes constantly in ordinary play.
**Partial (expected, fine):** fires for bathysphere and injection, not the rescue.

### If it fails
Open M3-S1. **Skip S3 entirely** — nothing to harden. Append the dead end to
`docs/INVARIANTS.md` *with the log evidence* and add it to the graveyard in
`CLAUDE.md`.

### Do not
Gate any behaviour yet — not HUD, camera or input · write to `bHideHUD` (it would
fight the game's own logic and confound M2-S2) · log per-frame · re-derive a signal
from pitch, timing, draw counts or view-target identity if this comes back negative
(graves 1–5, 8).

## M1-S3 · Harden into `EngineBridge`

**Status:** not started · **Type:** hardening, no visible change · **1 cycle** ·
**Status:** ⛔ **skipped — S2 returned no**, so there is nothing to harden. The
measured offsets are not lost: they are recorded in `docs/ENGINE-MAP.md`, and
`EngineBridge` should be built when a signal that actually works needs it.
· **Depends on:** S2 returning yes or partial. **Skip entirely if S2 returned no.**

Extra context: `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md` § *Tier 0* · `docs/modules/config.md` ·
`docs/STYLE.md` (this session writes a new file) · `docs/INVARIANTS.md` §
*Engine access*.

### The one idea
Promote the two proven offsets from a probe into `Game/EngineBridge.cpp` — a named
Tier 0 accessor layer with INI-overridable offsets, fail-closed identity checks and
correct lifetime.

### Specification

**1. New `Game/EngineBridge.cpp` / `.h`.** Small — a layer, not a framework:
- A guarded typed read. Every dereference guarded; a failed read returns a
  documented sentinel and logs **once**, never spams.
- An identity check: non-null, vtable inside the module, back-reference test.
- `EngineBridge_Cinematic()` returning **tri-state: true / false / unknown**.
  `unknown` is not `false` — consumers must distinguish "not in a cutscene" from
  "cannot tell", or M2 will silently gate features on a failed read.

**2. Offsets through config.** `struct VrConfig` in `Core/Config.h`, read in
`Config.cpp`, **visible in the startup echo** — the echo is the authority on what
took effect. INI overrides let a different storefront be corrected without a
rebuild, the standard already applied to `EnginePtrRva` and the `ArmHide` vtables.

**3. Lifetime.** **Clear every cached pointer the instant the pawn goes null** —
this is an invariant, and the `CurrentExorcismTarget` probe was previously polluted
by exactly this omission (a stale snapshot across a pawn change produced 18
meaningless transitions). Re-resolve on level load and save reload. Fix lifetime,
not range — do not time-limit the read to hide stale state.

**4.** Keep the `>>> CINE:` transition line, now naming its source.

### Falsifiable outcome
Full presentation route with: new config keys in the `=== BioshockVR config ===`
echo · `>>> CINE:` still bracketing the bathysphere exactly as in S2 · **no
`unknown` during ordinary play** · a clean re-resolve logged after a level load and
a save reload · no new warnings beyond the two pre-existing `C4244` in
`CameraHook.cpp`.

### If it fails
`unknown` in normal play means the identity check is too strict or the lifetime
reset fires spuriously. Fix that before M2. **Do not loosen the identity check to
make the symptom go away** — fail-closed is the invariant, and a false `true`
propagates into four features.

### Do not
Gate behaviour (M2-S1 is the first session allowed to change what the user sees) ·
build Tier 1/2 scaffolding "while you're in here" · refactor `GameState.cpp` beyond
adding the calls (StateBus is M2-S1) · bulk-reformat anything.
